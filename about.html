Why good coaches stay empty

Coaching skill and client numbers are barely related. That's uncomfortable, and it's the whole problem.

How we work

Narrow, talk to people, charge properly

Nearly every stuck coach is too broad. “I help people reach their potential” describes nothing a person searches for or recognizes as their own situation, so nobody responds.

The second issue is volume of conversations. Ten a week, consistently, changes a practice. Posting on social media instead feels productive and usually isn't, at least at the start.

The third is price. Underpricing attracts clients who don't commit and leaves you resentful within three months. We set prices from capacity and real numbers instead of nerves.

Our principles

Three beliefs behind the method

Narrow until it's uncomfortable

A specific problem for a specific group is what gets recognized.

Conversations, not content

Early clients come from people, not from posting.

No credential, no income claims

We're not a certifying body and we don't promise numbers.
