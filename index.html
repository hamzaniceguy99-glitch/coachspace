How we work

No certification, no income claims

Plenty of people sell coaches a “certification” and a promise of six figures. We issue no credential — we're not a certifying body — and we make no income claims, because nobody honest can.

What we offer is ordinary business help for a specific trade: say what you do clearly, talk to enough people, charge properly, and run sessions that change something. Most coaches who stall are missing one of those four.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Do I get a certification?

No. We are not a certifying body and issue no credential. If you want accreditation, look at established bodies — we'll happily point you at them.

Do I need a certification to coach?

In the US, coaching is largely unregulated, so generally no. But certain claims — therapy, medical, nutrition, financial advice — are regulated, and we'll help you stay clearly on the right side of that line.

Will I make six figures?

We don't make income claims and we'd be suspicious of anyone who does. Plenty of successful coaches run a good part-time practice, which is a perfectly fine outcome.

What kind of coaches do you work with?

Life, career, health-habit, business and specialist skill coaches. The business problems are remarkably similar across all of them.

Can you write my client agreement?

No — that's legal work. We'll tell you what typically belongs in one so your conversation with a lawyer is short and cheap.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.

What's actually stopping the clients?

Twenty minutes on your offer and where people drop off. Free, and specific.
